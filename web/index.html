What's inside

Built around how money actually moves.

Every screen maps to a real action on your accounts — nothing is a spreadsheet formula in disguise.

Three account types

Bank, credit and virtual accounts each carry their own rules — credit accounts track a limit and available credit, the rest track a straightforward balance.

Move money with intent

Income, expenses and transfers are logged as distinct, auditable actions instead of one generic entry that hides what actually happened.

Categorized by default

Groceries, rent, transport, salary, bonus — every entry sits under a category, so where your money goes shows up on its own.

Budgets that talk back

Set a limit per category and watch a progress bar warn you before you go over, not after.

Goals with a finish line

Ring-fence savings toward a target amount and track how close you are, right alongside your everyday accounts.

Your ledger, guarded

Password-protected accounts keep every balance and transaction private to you, and only you.
